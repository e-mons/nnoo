# NNOO Four-Tranche Build Plan

## Tranche 1 — 1 to 7 August 2026
**Status:** **ACCEPTED & PROTECTED**

Goal: establish the working foundation.
- Monorepo and quality-tool foundation.
- Single Next.js web shell.
- Single Expo mobile shell.
- Shared packages and contracts.
- Supabase environments, MCP workflow, migrations, types, and RLS base.
- Authentication and session handling.
- Marketing pages and working enquiries.
- Business onboarding.
- Owner/staff membership and role foundation.
- Initial NNOO admin area.
- Initial tests and deployment checks.

## Tranche 2 — 8 to 14 August 2026
**Status:** **ACCEPTED & PROTECTED**

Goal: support daily business operations.
- Dashboard.
- Financial record and journal foundation.
- Sales and expenses.
- Products, services, and stock movements.
- Low stock, batch, and expiry where applicable.
- Customers and suppliers.
- Invoices, receipts, and invoice payments.
- Shared reports and calculations.
- Paystack subscription billing.
- Administration for businesses, plans, subscriptions, and payments.
- Equivalent approved mobile workflows.

## Tranche 3 — 15 to 22 August 2026
**Status:** **ACCEPTED & PROTECTED**

Goal: turn records into understandable intelligence.
- AI Bookkeeper suggestion and confirmation flow.
- Verified daily, weekly, and monthly summaries.
- Ask NNOO with controlled data tools.
- Business Health Score v1 (`business-health-score-v1`).
- Credit Passport v1 (immutable snapshots, PDF, sharing).
- Notifications & Attention Center.
- Approved Meta WhatsApp Business integration.
- AI and scoring administration oversight.
- Inngest durable job automation foundation.

## Tranche 4 — 23 to 30 August 2026
**Status:** **ACTIVE**

Goal: complete, secure, test, deploy, and hand over the agreed release.
- **T4-P01:** Final System Audit, Completion Gap Analysis & Release Scope Freeze (Completed)
- **T4-P02:** Complete Platform Admin & Operational Management
- **T4-P03:** Production Security, Privacy & Access Hardening
- **T4-P04:** Data Protection, Backup, Restore & Disaster Recovery
- **T4-P05:** Reliability, Observability & Incident Management
- **T4-P06:** Performance, Scalability & Production Optimization
- **T4-P07:** Production Environment, Secrets & Provider Configuration
- **T4-P08:** Web Production Deployment & Release Engineering
- **T4-P09:** Mobile Production Build, EAS & Store Readiness
- **T4-P10:** Production External Integration Validation
- **T4-P11:** Full-System Final Regression, UAT & Go-Live Rehearsal
- **T4-P12:** Documentation, Operations & Project Handover
- **T4-P13:** Final Tranche 4 & August Delivery Acceptance

Completion: the agreed August release is reviewed, deployable, documented, and handed over.

## Excluded unless separately approved

Direct lending, full loan marketplace, bank Credit Intelligence API, government dashboard, automatic tax filing, full payroll, USSD, SMS application, voice IVR, full local-language voice recognition, advanced fraud ML, full offline sync for every feature, nine complete vertical editions, multi-country regulatory systems, and white-label deployments.
