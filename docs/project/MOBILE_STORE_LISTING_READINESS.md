## 2. Full Store Description (Factual & Verified Copy)

```text
NNOO is Africa’s AI Business Operating System — empowering merchants, wholesalers, retailers, and growing enterprises to manage daily commerce, double-entry accounting, and smart intelligence in one unified platform.

CORE BUSINESS CAPABILITIES:
• Sales & Point of Sale: Record cash and credit transactions instantly with server-verified calculations in exact minor currency units (kobo/cents).
• Inventory & Stock Receipts: Track inventory valuation, movements, low-stock thresholds, and reorder alerts in real time.
• Expense Management: Classify operating expenses, utility bills, and supplier payouts with complete audit trails.
• Invoicing & Digital Receipts: Generate professional branded PDF invoices and receipts for customers.
• Multi-Role Team Collaboration: Assign fine-grained roles (Owner, Manager, Cashier, Accountant) with strict permission isolation.

AI INTELLIGENCE & SMART TOOLS:
• AI Bookkeeper: Review suggested transaction classifications with manual human confirmation before ledger application.
• Smart Insights: View deterministic summaries of sales trends, expense ratios, and gross margin movements.
• Ask NNOO Assistant: Conversational business advisor answering queries about your catalog and operational history.
• Business Health Indicator: An internal operational indicator evaluating working capital, inventory velocity, and debt-to-income balance.
• Credit Passport: Share verified operational performance snapshots with trusted trade partners and commercial lenders.
• Priority Attention Center: Stay notified on stock shortages, overdue receivables, and critical business tasks.

SECURITY & DATA INTEGRITY:
• Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation: Your data is protected by enterprise-grade PostgreSQL Row Level Security.
• End-to-End Encryption: All communications and data transmissions use modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
• Zero Uncontrolled Financial Side Effects: Calculations use exact integer arithmetic with zero floating-point rounding errors.

NNOO is built for Nigerian and African businesses seeking clarity, control, and scalable growth.
```

## 3. Store Compliance & Claim Disclaimers

1. **Business Health Score Disclaimer:**
   - *Policy Statement:* The NNOO Business Health Score is an internal operational health indicator derived deterministically from business ledger records. It is not an official banking credit score, government rating, or guaranteed loan approval guarantee.
2. **Credit Passport Disclaimer:**
   - *Policy Statement:* Credit Passport provides verified operational performance snapshots of business activity. It does not constitute a statutory credit bureau report or formal government certificate.
3. **AI Accuracy Statement:**
   - *Policy Statement:* AI suggestions generated by Ask NNOO and AI Bookkeeper are assistive operational aids requiring human confirmation before financial mutation. Calculations are deterministically computed by NNOO’s domain engine.

## 6. App Reviewer Access & Testing Strategy

### Apple & Google Review Credentials:
- **Reviewer Account Strategy:** A dedicated non-production demonstration business account is provisioned for store review teams.
- **Reviewer User:** `reviewer@nnoo.app`
- **Assigned Business:** `NNOO Demo Enterprise` (Synthetic trade data: 5 sample products, 3 sample sales, 2 invoices).
- **Security Guard:** Zero customer financial data accessible. Platform Admin routes strictly inaccessible.
- **Review Notes for Apple:**
  - *Authentication:* Reviewer can log in with provided test account.
  - *Push Notifications:* Push registration can be tested in-app via Settings &rarr; Notifications.
  - *Account Deletion:* Can be initiated in Settings &rarr; Account & Privacy &rarr; Request Account Deletion.
- **Review Notes for Google (App Access):**
  - *Restricted Access:* All business operational features require sign-in using the provided test account. Full functionality is available without payment.
