## 1. Welcome to NNOO

NNOO is your all-in-one AI Business Operating System. This guide explains how to manage your daily sales, inventory, expenses, staff, and financial intelligence with zero accounting background required.

---

## 2. Daily Business Operations

### 2.1 Managing Products & Inventory
1. Navigate to **Inventory** $\to$ **Products** $\to$ **"Add Product"**.
2. Enter the Product Name, SKU/Barcode, Selling Price, and Cost Price.
3. Toggle **"Track Inventory"** to enable automatic stock tracking and reorder alerts.
4. To add stock, click **"Receive Stock"** and enter the quantity received.

### 2.2 Recording Sales & Issuing Receipts
1. Navigate to **Sales** $\to$ **"New Sale"**.
2. Select or add a customer (or choose "Walk-in Customer").
3. Add products and quantities. NNOO automatically calculates totals and cost of goods sold.
4. Select the payment status:
   - **Paid in Full:** Generates a downloadable PDF receipt.
   - **Credit Sale / Partial:** Adds the remaining balance to the customer's Accounts Receivable.

### 2.3 Processing Customer Payments & Invoices
1. Navigate to **Invoices** $\to$ **"New Invoice"** to send formal payment requests to corporate clients.
2. When payment arrives, open the invoice and click **"Record Payment"**.

### 2.4 Recording Business Expenses
1. Navigate to **Expenses** $\to$ **"Record Expense"**.
2. Enter the amount, select an expense category (Rent, Utilities, Fuel, Logistics), and choose Paid or Unpaid (AP liability).

## 3. Smart Business Intelligence & AI Tools

### 3.1 AI Bookkeeper (Receipt Intake)
1. Navigate to **Bookkeeper**.
2. Type or paste a transaction description (e.g. *"Bought 2 drums of diesel for ₦80,000 from TotalEnergies"*).
3. The AI Bookkeeper classifies the transaction and suggests the appropriate expense category.
4. **Review & Confirm:** Check the suggested fields, edit if necessary, and click **"Confirm & Post"**.

### 3.2 Smart Insights
- Every week, NNOO analyzes your sales patterns and generates 3 clear, actionable recommendations to improve your margins and reduce waste.

### 3.3 Ask NNOO
- Open the **Ask NNOO** chat widget on Web or Mobile.
- Ask questions in plain English (e.g. *"What were my total sales this week?"*, *"Which items are low in stock?"*, *"How much do my customers owe me?"*).

### 3.4 Business Health Score (0–100)
- View your score on the dashboard.
- Measures 4 pillars: **Profitability (30%)**, **Cash Flow & Liquidity (30%)**, **Inventory Efficiency (25%)**, and **Record Consistency (15%)**.

### 3.5 Credit Passport
1. Navigate to **Credit Passport** $\to$ **"Generate Snapshot"**.
2. Download a verified PDF report or generate a secure public link to share with suppliers, banks, or commercial partners.

## 4. Settings, WhatsApp & Team Management

- **Team Members:** Navigate to **Settings** $\to$ **Team** to invite managers, accountants, sales staff, and inventory officers.
- **WhatsApp Integration:** Navigate to **Settings** $\to$ **WhatsApp** $\to$ click **"Link WhatsApp"** $\to$ send the 6-digit code to our WhatsApp bot.
- **Subscription Billing:** Navigate to **Settings** $\to$ **Billing** to manage your NNOO plan (Starter, Growth, Scale) securely via Paystack.
