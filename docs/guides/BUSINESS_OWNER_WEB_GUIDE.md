# The Official NNOO Web Guide for Business Owners
**The Exact Step-by-Step Manual for Running Your Store from A to Z on NNOO**

---

## Welcome to NNOO!

Think of **NNOO** as your personal, super-smart shop manager who sits in your store 24 hours a day, 7 days a week:
- It records every single naira that comes in and goes out.
- It counts every product on your shelf so nothing goes missing or gets stolen.
- It remembers every customer who owes you money and helps you track their debts.
- It calculates your real profit so you never have to guess whether your business is growing.
- It produces official, verified bank reports so you can secure loans without paperwork.

You do **not** need any computer skills, accounting background, or technical knowledge. If you know how to send a WhatsApp message, you can master NNOO easily.

# Part 1: First-Time Setup (Registration & Onboarding)

You only need to complete this setup **once**. After this, your store is permanently open!

```
[ Step 1: Sign Up ] ➔ [ Step 2: Confirm Email ] ➔ [ Step 3: Create Business ] ➔ [ Step 4: Enter Dashboard ]
```

---

### Step 1: Create Your Account (`/sign-up`)
1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, or Microsoft Edge) on your phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Go to your NNOO web address and click the bright green **"Sign Up"** or **"Get Started"** button.
3. You will see the **Create your account** screen. Enter your details:
   - **First Name:** Type your first name *(e.g., Jane)*.
   - **Last Name:** Type your surname *(e.g., Doe)*.
   - **Email Address:** Type your personal or business email *(e.g., name@business.com)*.
   - **Password:** Type a secret password (must be at least 8 characters). Click the eye icon if you want to see what you typed.
   - **Confirm Password:** Type the exact same password again so you are certain there are no mistakes.
   - **Terms & Conditions:** Tick the small square checkbox: *"I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy"*.
4. Click the bright green button: **"CREATE ACCOUNT"**.

---

### Step 2: Confirm Your Email (`/verify-email`)
1. If prompted to verify your email, open your email inbox.
2. Open the email message sent by **NNOO**.
3. Click the confirmation link inside. Your account is now verified!

---

### Step 3: Set Up Your Business Store (`/onboarding`)
Next, NNOO will display the **"Set up your business"** screen. Fill in these 3 fields:
1. **Business Name:** Type the exact name of your shop or company.  
   *(Example: Lagos Wholesale & Stores, Divine Grace Boutique, or Kano Farm Supplies)*
2. **Industry:** Click the dropdown box and select your exact line of business:
   - **Market Trader** (General provision stores, market stalls, traders)
   - **Pharmacy** (Chemist shops, medical stores, pharmacies)
   - **Restaurant** (Eateries, food canteens, cafes, bakeries)
   - **School** (Creches, primary, secondary, or training schools)
   - **Church** (Places of worship, ministries)
   - **Farm** (Poultry, livestock, fisheries, crop farming)
   - **Hotel** (Guest houses, hotels, hospitality)
   - **Transport** (Logistics, delivery, haulage, transport services)
   - **Manufacturing** (Block industries, bakeries, craft makers, factories)
   - **Other** (Any other business)
3. **Business Phone (Optional):** Enter your business phone number *(e.g., `+234 801 234 5678`)*.
4. Click the bright green button: **"Create Business"** *(it will show "Setting up..." while it prepares your workspace)*.

🎉 **You are done!** NNOO sets up your private database workspace and immediately opens your **Main Store Dashboard**.

# Part 4: How to Use Every Hub from A to Z

---

## Hub 1: 💰 Money & Sales (`/money`)

This hub manages all cashflow in and out of your business.

### Overview Strip (At the Top of Money Hub):
- **Total Money In:** Total cash and payments collected from sales.
- **Total Money Out:** Total money paid out for expenses.
- **Net Cash Remaining:** Total Money In minus Total Money Out.
- **Who Owes You (Invoices):** Total money locked in unpaid customer invoices.

### Top Action Buttons:
- **`+ Record Sale (Money In)`** ➔ Record customer purchases.
- **`- Record Expense (Money Out)`** ➔ Record money spent from the drawer or bank.
- **`✨ AI Quick Record`** ➔ Record any transaction using plain everyday words.

### The 6 Tabs in Money Hub:
1. **📋 All Activity:** A unified list of every sale and expense in order of time.
2. **🛒 Sales History:** Every sale made, customer name, date, amount, and payment status.
3. **📄 Invoices:** Outstanding, paid, and overdue bills issued to customers.
4. **💳 Expenses:** All business spending sorted by category.
5. **🧾 Receipts:** Official digital receipts generated for customers.
6. **✨ AI Bookkeeper:** Automatic transaction classification awaiting your one-click approval.

---

### Step-by-Step 1: How to Record a Sale (`+ Record Sale`)
1. Click the green button: **`+ Record Sale (Money In)`**.
2. **Select Customer:**
   - Choose an existing customer from your dropdown, or leave it blank if they are a walk-in customer.
   - **Crucial Rule:** If the customer is not paying in full today (taking goods on credit / pay later), **you must select a customer** so NNOO knows who owes the money!
3. **Add Items to Cart:**
   - Select an item from your product catalog.
   - Type the **Quantity** (how many they are buying).
   - Type any **Discount** if you gave them a reduced price.
4. **Record Payment:**
   - Click the quick button for full payment, or type a custom amount.
   - Choose the **Payment Method**:
     - **Cash** (Physical paper money)
     - **Bank Transfer** (Direct bank transfer)
     - **POS** (Card swipe on your terminal)
     - **Other** (Any other method)
   - If the customer pays only half, enter the partial amount paid. The rest is saved automatically as credit debt!
5. Click **"Complete Sale"**.
6. A digital receipt is generated instantly with its unique receipt number! You can print it or share it on WhatsApp.

---

### Step-by-Step 2: How to Record an Expense (`- Record Expense`)
1. Click the red button: **`- Record Expense (Money Out)`**.
2. **Category *:** Select what the expense was for *(e.g., Shop Rent, Fuel, Transport, Wages, Packaging, Utilities)*.
3. **Supplier:** Select which vendor you paid *(or leave as "None (Walk-up)")*.
4. **Total Amount (NGN) *:** Type how much you spent *(e.g., 5000)*.
5. **Date *:** The date the money was spent (defaults to today).
6. **Description *:** Type a quick note *(e.g., "Purchased 5 litres of petrol for shop generator")*.
7. **Initial Payment:**
   - **Amount Paid Now:** How much you paid right away.
   - **Payment Method:** Cash, Bank Transfer, POS, or Other.
8. **Attach Receipt Photo (Optional):** Upload a photo of the paper receipt from your phone camera.
9. Click **"Record Expense"**.

---

### Step-by-Step 3: How to Create an Invoice (`/invoices/new`)
1. In Money Hub, click the **📄 Invoices** tab, then click **"+ Create Invoice"**.
2. **Select Customer *:** Pick the customer from the dropdown list.
3. **Due Date:** Choose the date by which the customer must pay.
4. **Notes / Terms:** Enter any payment instructions or bank account details.
5. **Add Items:** Pick the products/services, enter quantities, and discounts.
6. Click **"Save & Issue Invoice"**.
7. The invoice is now officially issued.
8. **When the customer pays:** Open that invoice, click **"Record Payment"**, enter the amount received, and the invoice changes to **"Paid"**!

---

### Step-by-Step 4: Using the AI Bookkeeper (`✨ AI Bookkeeper`)
- NNOO continuously sorts transactions into double-entry accounting records in the background.
- Click the **✨ AI Bookkeeper** tab.
- You will see suggestions for your recent sales and expenses.
- Review the suggestion and click the green **"Approve"** button.
- Your store ledgers are now permanently clean and audit-ready without hiring an expensive firm!

## Hub 2: 📦 Items & Stock (`/stock`)

This hub manages all products, prices, live inventory quantities, and prevents stock theft.

### Top Summary Cards:
- **Total Items for Sale:** Number of products and services listed.
- **Total Units on Hand:** Total physical pieces counted across all shelves.
- **Total Stock Value:** Total purchase cost value of all goods in your shop.
- **Low / Out of Stock:** Number of items that need urgent reordering.

### The 4 Tabs in Items & Stock:
1. **📦 All Items & Prices:** Master catalog of everything you sell.
2. **⚠️ Low Stock Alert:** Urgent warning list of products running out.
3. **🏷️ Categories:** Product groups (e.g., Beverages, Toiletries, Electronics).
4. **🚚 Stock Receipts & History:** Records of past supplier deliveries.

---

### Step-by-Step 1: How to Add a New Item (`+ Add New Item`)
1. Click the green button: **`+ Add New Item`**.
2. **Item Type:** Choose **Product** (physical goods) or **Service** (labor, repair, consulting).
3. **Name *:** Type the product name *(e.g., Peak Milk 400g Tin)*.
4. **Description:** Any extra details *(e.g., Full cream milk tin)*.
5. **Category:** Choose a category *(e.g., Groceries)*.
6. **Unit Code *:** How it is measured *(e.g., item, piece, carton, bag, kg - defaults to "item")*.
7. **SKU / Barcode (Optional):** Enter barcode numbers or product codes.
8. **Selling Price *:** How much you sell one unit to customers *(e.g., `1500.00`)*.
9. **Cost Price:** How much you bought one unit from your supplier *(e.g., `1200.00`)*.
10. **Track Inventory:** Leave the box ticked so NNOO tracks your stock count automatically!
11. Click **"Create Item"**.

---

### Step-by-Step 2: How to Receive New Stock (`+ Receive Stock Shipment`)
When a delivery van or supplier brings new inventory to your shop:
1. Click **`+ Receive Stock Shipment`**.
2. **Select Supplier *:** Choose which supplier delivered the goods.
3. **Date & Reference:** The arrival date and supplier's waybill/invoice number.
4. **Add Items:** Select the product from the dropdown, type the **Quantity Received**, and the **Unit Cost**.
5. **Payment Section:**
   - If you paid the supplier in full, click **"Pay Full Balance"** and choose Cash, Bank Transfer, or POS.
   - If the supplier gave you goods on credit, leave the payment empty. NNOO will remember what you owe them!
6. Click **"Record Stock Receipt"**.
7. Your shelf stock counts increase automatically!

---

### Step-by-Step 3: What to Do If Goods Spoil or Break (`Adjust Stock`)
If an item breaks, expires, or goes missing:
1. In the **All Items & Prices** tab, find the item and click **"Adjust Stock"**.
2. Choose the reason: **Damaged**, **Expired**, **Missing / Theft**, or **Recount Correction**.
3. Type how many units were lost.
4. Click **"Apply Adjustment"**. Your inventory count updates cleanly, and the loss is noted in your accounting records.

4. Enter how much they are paying today (they can pay in full or in parts).

## Hub 5: ⚙️ Store Settings (`/settings`)

Manage your store details, staff access, WhatsApp, and subscription.

On the left side of Settings, click any of the 6 sections:

### 1. Store Details (`/settings`)
- Update your **Business Name**, **Legal Name**, **Industry**, **Currency Code (NGN)**, **Timezone**, **Contact Email**, **Phone Number**, and **Physical Address**. Click **"Save Settings"**.

### 2. Staff & Helpers (`/settings/team`)
Never give staff your personal password!
1. Under **Invite New Member**, enter your worker's **Email Address**.
2. Select their **Role**:
   - **Sales Staff:** Can only record sales, look up prices, and issue receipts. They **cannot** see your overall profits, bank balances, or expenses.
   - **Inventory Staff:** Can receive stock and count items.
   - **Manager:** Can manage products, stock, sales, and expenses.
   - **Accountant:** Can review ledgers and view financial reports.
   - **Administrator:** Can manage everything except business ownership.
3. Click **"Invite Member"**.
4. An invitation link is generated immediately so you can copy and send it directly to your worker.

### 3. WhatsApp Business (`/settings/whatsapp`)
Connect NNOO to your WhatsApp so you never miss an alert:
1. Click the green button: **`🔗 Connect WhatsApp`**.
2. NNOO displays your 10-minute code:  
   `CONNECT <6-digit-code>`
3. Click the button that says **`💬 Open WhatsApp Chat`**.
4. Send the pre-typed code to the official NNOO WhatsApp number.
5. Within 5 seconds, your screen updates to **Connected** with a green pulse badge!
6. Choose which alerts you want to receive on WhatsApp:
   - *Inventory & Stock Alerts*
   - *Invoice & Receivables Alerts*
   - *AI Bookkeeper Reviews*
   - *Business Health Score Updates*
   - *Credit Passport Updates*

### 4. Automations & Schedules (`/settings/automations`)
- Manage your automated schedules for:
  - **Verified Business Summary** (Daily morning briefings)
  - **Business Health Score Refresh** (Weekly score checks)
  - **Attention Condition Scanner** (Immediate alerts for low stock or overdue debts)

### 5. Plan & Billing (`/settings/billing`)
- View your current subscription plan.
- Click **"Upgrade Plan"** to move to Starter, Growth, or Pro.

### 6. Attention & Alerts (`/settings/notifications`)
- Review operational, security, and financial notices requiring your attention.

# Part 6: Quick Answers to Common Questions

- **Q: What if I forget my password?**  
  On the Sign In screen, click **"Forgot?"** next to the Password label. Enter your email address and click the link sent to your inbox to set a new password.
- **Q: Can my sales boy or cashier see my profits or expenses?**  
  **No.** When you invite them with the **Sales Staff** role, they only see the selling screen and product catalog. They can never see your cost prices, profit margins, expenses, or bank balances.
- **Q: Can I use this on my mobile phone?**  
  **Yes.** Open Google Chrome or Safari on any smartphone, go to your NNOO web address, and use it just like an app.
- **Q: What if I have more than one shop or business?**  
  You can create and manage multiple businesses under your single login and switch between them anytime from the top menu or on WhatsApp by typing `BUSINESS`.
